# Garrison watch

Follow `docs/watches/garrison.md`: the standing procedure for watching the running garrison toward the operator's goals.

The goals are in `C:/mc-state/garrison/watch-goals.json`; the last passes are in `C:/mc-state/garrison/watch-log.md`.
Read both, then `git log --oneline -8`, `git status` and the last line of `C:/mc-state/garrison/runs.log`, and carry on
from there. Live garrison: `http://127.0.0.1:8125`; logs: `C:/mc-state/garrison/logs`.
