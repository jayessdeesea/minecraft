# Boats

## Why
Soldiers swim across water at half walking pace, unable to fight or sprint while afloat. A wide
lake or a stretch of sea is slow and exposed to cross that way; a boat crosses it at several times
walking pace and keeps the soldier out of the water.

## Done when
- A squad ordered across water wider than a set distance crafts boats (five planks each), places
  them at the bank, crosses, and leaves them - shown in a scenario on a lake too wide to swim in
  good time.

## Notes
Needs the protocol for using an item on water (placing the boat), interacting with an entity
(boarding), the vehicle-steering and vehicle-move packets, and dismounting. The planner would
price a water crossing by boat against swimming and walking round.
