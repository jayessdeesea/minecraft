# The wider doctrine catalogue

## Why
Most of infantry small-unit doctrine has a plausible Minecraft analogue. The experiment is to see
which pieces carry over to LLM-led squads. This item is the list of candidates; each graduates to
its own backlog item when it's picked up.

## Candidates
- **Consolidation and reorganisation after contact.** Re-establish security, report LACE, re-form
  the chain of command. Redistribution is backlog/cross-leveling.md.
- **Security halts.** After a long move, the squad stops in a perimeter with sectors of fire rather
  than in a line. The overnight version is backlog/patrol-base.md.
- **Rules of engagement.** When soldiers may attack: hostiles only, never players, and passive
  mobs only when ordered to hunt (backlog/subsistence.md). This is an explicit, testable policy
  rather than whatever `HostileMobRegistry` happens to contain.
- **Squad column and movement techniques** (ATP 3-21.8 ¶3-53; traveling, traveling overwatch,
  bounding overwatch). Bounding means one element moves while another watches, so it needs more
  than one fire team. Weapons placement within the formation falls under this item too.
- **Battle drills** beyond react to contact (docs/military-doctrine.md §8): break contact, and
  others. These are standardised responses, which fit the scenario harness as fixed test cases.
- **Casualty reporting.** Deaths roll up as a casualty report rather than a CANTCO.
