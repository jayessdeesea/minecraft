# The wider doctrine catalogue

## Why
Most of infantry small-unit doctrine has a plausible Minecraft analogue, and some of it isn't tried yet. This item is
the list of candidates; each graduates to its own backlog item when it's picked up.

## What's left
- **Security halts.** After a long move, the squad stops in a perimeter with sectors of fire rather
  than in a line. The overnight version is the patrol base (docs/military-doctrine.md § 14).
- **Rules of engagement.** When soldiers may attack - hostiles only, never players, and passive mobs only when hunting
  for food - as an explicit, tested policy rather than whatever `HostileMobRegistry` happens to contain. No doc states
  it today.
- **Squad column and movement techniques** (ATP 3-21.8 ¶3-53; traveling, traveling overwatch,
  bounding overwatch). Bounding means one element moves while another watches, so it needs more
  than one fire team. Weapons placement within the formation falls under this item too.
- **Battle drills** beyond react to contact (docs/military-doctrine.md § 8), as fixed cases in the scenario harness.
  Break contact is backlog/engagement-doctrine.md's.
- **Reorganisation after contact:** report LACE, re-form the chain of command. A soldier's own consolidation - back to
  its order after a fight - is backlog/soldier-order.md; redistribution is backlog/cross-leveling.md.

## Done when
Each candidate has its own backlog item or is dropped, and this file is deleted.
