# Subsistence: rations the chain of command can see and share

## Why
Soldiers eat what they carry and hunt when they have nothing (docs/military-doctrine.md § 4), but
the squad leaders and the general can't see who has food: the LOGSTAT doesn't count rations, and
one soldier with a stack of meat beside one starving can't share it.

## Done when
- Rations show in the LOGSTAT (they're in each soldier's LACE as `Kit.rations`).
- They can be cross-levelled (backlog/cross-leveling.md).

## Notes
- **Biome dependence** (see backlog/renewables.md): plains have cows and sheep, deserts almost
  nothing, and the Nether's hoglins fight back.
- Cooking can wait: raw meat feeds, only less.
