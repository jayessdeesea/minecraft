# Keep the squad together

## Why
In live runs the squad scatters and stays scattered. One run had Alice at the objective, Bob stuck
in a cave 50 blocks below (y=31, CANTCO) and Carol on her cliff ledge about 150 blocks away. The
point man keeps advancing and the formation slots move with him, whether or not anyone can follow.
So the react-to-contact drill had nobody within 24 blocks to send when three zombies attacked
Alice, and she died alone. Doctrine expects a leader to keep elements within supporting distance:
halt, or adjust the pace, until the unit closes up.

## Done when
- The squad leader can see dispersal: the prompt states the spread, for example "Bob is 60 blocks
  from the point man and can't reach his slot".
- Something acts on it. Either a deterministic rule holds the point man while too many soldiers
  are out of supporting distance, or the squad leader is expected to order a halt. Which one is
  the design question.
- A scenario with obstacles that split the squad shows it re-forming before it moves on.
