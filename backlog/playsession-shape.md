# What else should `PlaySession` expose?

## Why
`PlaySession` covers position reports, attacks and respawn. Chat has been raised but not decided.
It could carry squad radio traffic in-game, visible to a human watching, or let an operator issue
missions.

## Decision needed
Whether soldiers or squad leaders should speak in game chat. If they should, whether that is
display-only (the radio net mirrored to chat) or an input channel too.
