# More of the materials economy

## Why
Squads gather wood and stone where the general sends them, make the tools they need, and stockpile
what they gather at the supply point (docs/military-doctrine.md § 12). A soldier mines iron ore in
reach for its own kit (§ 11), and a MINE squad cuts stone on its way down (§ 16; ore at depth is
backlog/mining.md). Coal, and building materials like dirt and sand, aren't gathered.
Sharing what's gathered between soldiers is backlog/cross-leveling.md.

## Done when
- At least one more material is gathered as a squad task: coal ore (which digs with the wooden
  pickaxe, and makes torches without a furnace), or sand and dirt.
