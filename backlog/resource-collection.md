# More of the materials economy

## Why
Squads gather wood and stone where the general sends them, make the tools they need, and stockpile
what they gather at the supply point (docs/military-doctrine.md § 12). Wood and stone are the only
materials, so torches (coal), better tools (iron) and building (dirt, sand) are out of reach.
Sharing what's gathered between soldiers is backlog/cross-leveling.md.

## Done when
- At least one more material is gathered: coal ore (which digs with the wooden pickaxe, and is
  needed for torches in backlog/patrol-base.md), iron ore (with a stone pickaxe made from
  cobblestone), or sand and dirt.
