# Questions for the operator (overnight, 2026-11-16)

## The graduated slope (backlog/flatten-campaign.md)
The bench is three rows today, each a block over the one inside it (1:1). Making it as wide as
the cut is deep - a 13-high hill wants 13 rows - runs into what stands round the site:

- The depots stand 4 west of the edge and the dump 5 west; the keep's mine head is off the keep's
  north-west corner. Rows past 3 can dig under them.
- Each extra row on a hillside costs a wedge: rows 4 to 12 against a 12-high edge are 36 blocks a
  column, about 4,600 along the big site's 129-block side - more than the site's own dig estimate.

Proposal: rows out to 8, dug down 1:1 as now, leaving a berth of 6 round each depot stand and
the mine head (the slope goes round them). Past 8, the haul road is the way off.

What the answer changes: how wide the bench goes (8, the full depth, or another cap), and whether
depots and the mine head move out past it or the slope goes round them.

## Where the night shift mines once the keep's shaft is spent (backlog/mining.md)
The keep's first mine was cut to bedrock. Every move since has been costly: a site needs a fresh
shaft (54-104 steps, a night with no ore), and the ground round the keep has been poor - a lake
at x=-90, a well blocked by water at its 58th step, a hill over an open cave, a mountain top at
x=-184 (field346-355). A far site also costs the day: the squads walk back 150+ blocks to the keep
at dawn, and several were stranded in caves on the way (backlog/long-routes.md). Built tonight: the
next site is the nearest untried round the keep, every way, out to 192 blocks.

Options:
- A. Keep the night shift on one mine and go wider instead: run its tunnels and branches out
  further at the levels it has (the stretch cap is 96 blocks today), rather than moving.
- B. A standing second shaft beside the keep's first, dug by day by a spare squad, so a fresh
  shaft is ready before the old one is spent.
- C. As built: move to the nearest untried site when one is spent, accepting a night's cost each
  time.

What the answer changes: whether the night shift ever leaves the keep's mine, and how far
from the keep it may go.
