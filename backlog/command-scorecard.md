# The command scorecard: what the orders couldn't say, and a verdict

## Why
The dawn review scores each kind of task and order by soldier-time - the share under each report, CANTCO
reasons ranked, losses by area (`app.CommandScorecard`; docs/military-doctrine.md § 17). It doesn't count the
signs that an order is missing from the vocabulary, and it gives numbers with no verdict.

Each order's life as events is backlog/soldier-lineage.md's; an order's time against what was expected is
backlog/authority-and-missions.md, step 7.

## What's left
- **Gaps in the vocabulary, counted:** a squad leader or the platoon leader wanted something the orders
  couldn't say - intent prose that contradicts the order (a "gather wood" objective with task NONE), the same
  order given over and over, work done by accident.
- **A verdict in the dawn review:** what to keep and what to improve, beside the scores.
- **Closing the loop:** a bad score becomes a squad-simulation scenario; the order changes when a scenario
  can't pass without the change.

## Done when
- A garrison's dawn review lists the gaps counted, and a keep-or-improve verdict for each kind of order.
